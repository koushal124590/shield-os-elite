PROTOCOL_01 // VOIP_ENCRYPTION

The Quantum Core engine never records or logs your calls. Every voice packet is encrypted at the source using AES-256 and RSA-4096. Your conversations vanish into static the microsecond you hang up.

PROTOCOL_02 // EPHEMERAL_MESSAGING

Your messages are like shadows on the mesh. Once a communication is acknowledged, the Zero-Cache Eraser wipes it from both global nodes and local device memory. We maintain zero backups.

PROTOCOL_03 // ANONYMOUS_IDENTITY

We don't want to know who you are. Your Node ID is a randomized hash that changes frequently. No IP addresses are ever linked to your communications or your Node Credit (◈) balance.

PROTOCOL_04 // LEGAL_PROTECTION

Because SHIELD OS is decentralized, there is no central database to subpoena. Our legal protocol is simple: We physically cannot provide what we do not have. You are protected by the math of the universe.

PROTOCOL_05 // DATA_SOVEREIGNTY

Your private keys are generated on-device and never leave your hardware. You are the only person in existence who can access your encrypted vault. Even our developers are locked out.
